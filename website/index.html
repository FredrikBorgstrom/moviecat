Your movie files,
finally looking like a library.

MovieCat scans your folders for movies and TV shows, recognises each title from its file name, and shows the poster, rating, year and a short summary. Free for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Download MovieCat No account, no sign-up, no API key.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Works instantly

Add a folder and watch the posters appear. MovieCat uses free public movie databases, so there's nothing to sign up for.

Finds your movies for you

"Find movies automatically" searches your home folder and external drives and proposes the folders that hold films and series.

TV shows, grouped

Episodes are recognised by season and episode number and grouped per show, including anime-style numbering.

Leaves out the noise

Home videos, screen recordings, phone clips and tutorials stay out of your library, even in folders that mix them with movies.

Rate, search, sort

Give titles your own 1–10 rating, filter by movies or TV, and sort by title, year, rating or date modified.

Fix a match in seconds

If a title is wrong, search and pick the right one. MovieCat remembers your choice.

Messy file names? No problem.

MovieCat understands typical release names, folder layouts and even foreign titles.

  • The.Matrix.1999.1080p.BluRay.x264.mkvThe Matrix (1999)
  • Breaking.Bad.S01E02.720p.mkvBreaking Bad · S1 E2
  • Fargo/Season 2/S02E03.mkvFargo · S2 E3
  • Heat (1995)/movie.mkvHeat (1995)
  • Det.Sjunde.Inseglet.1957.mkvThe Seventh Seal (1957)

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your library stays on your computer; only the titles read from file names are sent to public movie databases to look them up. Read the privacy policy.

Download

Free and open source (MIT). Latest version from GitHub.

macOSMovieCat-macos.zip WindowsMovieCat-windows-x64-setup.exe LinuxMovieCat-x86_64.AppImage
  • macOS: the download isn't signed, so the first time, right-click MovieCat → Open. A Mac App Store version is on its way.
  • Windows: run the installer. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher: click More info → Run anyway. Prefer no installer? Get the zip.
  • Linux: make the AppImage executable (chmod +x MovieCat-x86_64.AppImage) and run it, or get the tar.gz (needs GTK 3).

Or use a package manager

  • Homebrew (macOS): brew install --cask fredrikborgstrom/tap/moviecat
  • Scoop (Windows): scoop bucket add moviecat https://github.com/FredrikBorgstrom/moviecat, then scoop install moviecat

Older versions are on the releases page.
